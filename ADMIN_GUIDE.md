# Web Desa Burujul Jaya - Admin Guide

Welcome to the Burujul Jaya administration panel.

## Dashboard
The dashboard provides a high-level overview of the system's statistics, including total published articles, UMKM products, tourism spots, and the most viewed content. 

## Content Management
- **Articles & News**: Manage village news. Supports Draft/Published statuses.
- **Announcements**: Highlight important notices for the community.
- **Village Pages**: Manage dynamic pages like Vision & Mission or Apparatus profiles.
- **Galleries**: Upload photos or link YouTube videos.

## Economy & Tourism
- **UMKM & Products**: Register local businesses (UMKM) and showcase their products to stimulate the local economy.
- **Tourism**: Manage tourism destinations and list available facilities.

## System Management
- **Settings**: Adjust Global SEO defaults, maintenance mode, and general village contact information.
- **Activity Logs**: View a trail of who created, updated, or deleted content.
- **Backups**: Download backups of your database.
- **Cache**: Clear the application cache if you encounter stale data.
- **Audits**: Monitor failed login attempts and permission changes for security.

### Tip: Roles and Permissions
Users can be assigned roles (e.g., `admin-desa`, `Super Admin`). Only Super Admins have access to the System Management module.
